Frequently Asked Questions

Supercar Pre-Purchase Intelligence FAQ

Everything you must know before transferring funds for a used Lamborghini.

How much does a Lamborghini carbon ceramic rotor replacement cost? ↓

A full four-corner Carbon Ceramic Brake (CCB) replacement on a Lamborghini Huracán or Aventador typically exceeds $24,000 to $28,000 at factory dealerships. Never rely on pad thickness alone; CCB discs lose mass through matrix oxidation. Demand an optical density (Carboteq) or dry weight in grams audit.

Why is the Front Axle Lift system mandatory on a used Huracán or Aventador? ↓

Exotic cars without the hydraulic nose lift suffer an immediate $10,000 to $12,000 resale penalty. Steep driveway scrapes ruin front carbon splitters and radiator housings. Missing lift is the #1 leverage buyers use to discount dealer asking prices.

What is the chronic clutch issue on Aventador ISR transmissions? ↓

The 7-speed Independent Shifting Rods (ISR) single-clutch transmission runs extreme hydraulic pressures. Release bearing binding and sensor misalignments cause slipping that costs $12,000 to $18,000 to replace. LamboSpec reviews E-Gear/ISR snap data to verify clutch life percentage.

How does LamboSpec write verbatim dealer counter-scripts? ↓

We aggregate live market comps from Bring a Trailer, Cars & Bids, and dealer networks. When an asking price exceeds verified comps or the vehicle lacks critical equipment, our engine writes professional, unemotional counter-offers that save buyers an average of $18,400.
